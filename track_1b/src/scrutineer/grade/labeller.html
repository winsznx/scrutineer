Scrutineer: human check

correct: right outcome (accepted / rejected) and no wrong fact; a yes share within 2 points counts as right. incorrect: wrong outcome, or any wrong fact (a yes share more than 2 points off). not attempted: no answer ("I don't know") and nothing wrong stated. A "majority of X % of the votes" for a rejected proposal means X % voted no, so the yes share is 100 − X. Every label is saved the moment you press a key.
